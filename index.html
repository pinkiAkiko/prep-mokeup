Do's & Don'ts

Do's

  • File quarterly returns within stipulated deadlines using Form E and Form F.
  • Report any loss, damage, or pilferage immediately to the NCB Zonal Office and on the PREP portal.
  • Store controlled substances in a locked, secure facility with restricted access.
  • Keep all licenses and approvals readily available for inspection by NCB officers at any time.

Don'ts

  • Do not sell or transfer controlled substances without prior written permission from NCB.
  • Do not exceed the approved consumption or production limits without obtaining prior authorization.
  • Do not destroy or dispose of controlled substances without obtaining prior permission from the Zonal NCB office.
  • Do not delay filing of quarterly returns — monetary and legal penalties apply for late submission.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PREP and who needs to register?
PREP (Precursor Register and Evaluation Portal) is the official online portal of the Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB), Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India. It is established under the NDPS Act, 1985 and the Regulation of Controlled Substances (RCS) Order, 2013. Any entity — manufacturer, trader, importer, exporter, or research institution — dealing in the 8 controlled substances must register and obtain a Unique Registration Number (URN).
What are the 8 controlled substances regulated under NDPS Act, 1985?
NCB regulates 8 controlled substances: (1) Opium derivatives — Morphine, Codeine, Thebaine, (2) Heroin (Diacetylmorphine), (3) Cannabis — Marijuana, Hashish/Charas, and Ganja, (4) Coca alkaloids — Cocaine, (5) Methamphetamine, (6) Amphetamine, (7) LSD (Lysergic Acid Diethylamide), and (8) Methaqualone.
What happens if a loss, theft, or damage of a controlled substance occurs?
Any loss, damage, or pilferage must be reported immediately to the NCB Zonal Office and recorded on the PREP portal using the incident reporting module. Failure to report within the stipulated time attracts penalties under the NDPS Act, 1985.
How do I apply for a URN (Unique Registration Number)?
Log in to the PREP portal and fill Form B (Application for URN Registration) with complete company details, list of controlled substances handled, and supporting documents. The concerned NCB Zonal Office will review it and issue Form A (URN Certificate) upon approval.
Can controlled substances be sold or transferred without NCB permission?
No. Sale or transfer of any controlled substance requires prior written permission from NCB. The buyer must hold a valid URN for the specific substance. All consignments must be accompanied by Form G (Consignment Note) and an intimation must be filed via Form H. Transfers without NCB authorisation are a punishable offence under the NDPS Act, 1985.
